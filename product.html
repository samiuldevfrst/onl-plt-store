Monstera Deliciosa

৳ 1,200

The Monstera is a beautiful tropical houseplant known for its large split leaves.

Plant Size

Light Requirement

Bright indirect sunlight is recommended.

Watering

Water when the top layer of soil becomes dry.

Toxicity

Monstera can be harmful if eaten by pets. Keep it away from cats and dogs.

Repotting

Repot approximately every 1–2 years when the plant becomes root-bound.
